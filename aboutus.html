HY-LINK FINANCE LIMITED is a dynamic and forward-thinking financial services company committed to creating access, unlocking opportunities, and empowering individuals, businesses, and communities to achieve sustainable financial growth.

Incorporated in 2021, HY-LINK Finance was established with a clear vision: to bridge the gap between financial opportunities and the people and businesses that need them most. Today, we have evolved into a growing financial services institution providing structured, accessible, and customer-focused solutions designed to support wealth creation, financial management, business growth, and long-term financial stability.

Our approach goes beyond providing financial products. We seek to build lasting relationships with our clients by understanding their unique financial needs and delivering solutions that are reliable, transparent, flexible, and responsive.

Regulatory standing

Built on trust.
Driven by opportunity.

Licensed lender Regulatory approvals Compliance framework

As part of our commitment to operating with the highest standards of professionalism, accountability, and regulatory compliance, HY-LINK FINANCE LIMITED is now duly certified as a lender and has obtained the necessary licences and regulatory approvals required for its lending operations.

This milestone represents an important step in our journey and reinforces our commitment to providing financial services within an appropriate regulatory and compliance framework. It also reflects our continuous investment in building an institution that our customers, partners, and stakeholders can confidently trust.

Through responsible lending, disciplined financial management, and innovative service delivery, we remain focused on creating meaningful financial opportunities while promoting sustainable growth for the individuals and businesses we serve.

The group

An integrated financial ecosystem

HY-LINK Finance operates within a growing network of complementary businesses, working closely with our sister companies, HY-LINK Multipurpose Cooperative Society and HY-LINK Global Services, to create an integrated ecosystem that connects financial services with real-world opportunities.

This integrated approach enables us to look beyond individual financial transactions and focus on the broader financial journey of our customers.
